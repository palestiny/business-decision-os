# BUSINESS OUTCOME & VERIFICATION DESIGN GATE

## Status

PASS — initial deterministic verification foundation

## Locked

- Business Outcome is separate from ActionExecution.
- Verification is separate from Outcome observation.
- Execution success does not imply business success.
- Expected outcome is explicit.
- Deterministic verification is preferred where possible.
- Unknown/inconclusive evidence is preserved as uncertainty.
- AI may explain but does not silently define truth.
- Decision Memory consumes verified results.

## Not yet locked

- Advanced metric expressions.
- Multi-metric formulas.
- Automatic collection schedules.
- Human override semantics.
- External analytics/provider contracts.

## Next implementation

Add Outcome and Verification domain primitives, persistence ports, commands, and tests before introducing automated collectors.
