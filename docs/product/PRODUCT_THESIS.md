# Product Thesis

## Product

**Business Decision Operating System (Decision OS)**

Decision OS is a business decision-and-execution layer, not another ERP.

ERP, PSA, CRM, HCM, BI, spreadsheets, and other systems remain systems of record and sources of context. Decision OS manages the gap between discovering a business issue and verifying whether the chosen response actually worked.

## Core problem

Organizations can often detect problems but still struggle to close the loop:

1. Signal is detected.
2. Evidence is collected manually.
3. Analysis is fragmented.
4. Options are discussed across meetings/messages.
5. A decision is made.
6. Approval may happen elsewhere.
7. Actions are executed in different systems.
8. Business outcome is measured later or not at all.
9. The organization loses the decision context.

The product targets this **Insight-to-Action Gap**.

## Core lifecycle

Signal → Case → Evidence → Analysis → Options → Decision → Approval → Action → Outcome → Verification → Learning

## Initial wedge

Project / Delivery Performance Decisions.

Initial case types:

- PROJECT_MARGIN_RISK
- RESOURCE_CAPACITY_RISK
- REVENUE_BILLING_LEAKAGE

Project profitability is a wedge, not the product identity.

## Product principles

- Decision OS complements systems of record.
- Human authority remains explicit.
- Decision and approval are separate concepts.
- Action completion is not business success.
- Outcomes require verification.
- AI may analyze and recommend but does not silently become authority.
- Business truth remains in the domain, not in AI output.
- Every important decision must be auditable.
- Integrations are ports/adapters, not domain dependencies.
- The first product must be useful without AI.

## Current hypotheses

The following are hypotheses, not validated facts:

- Mid-market project/service-oriented companies are the initial customer segment.
- Project/delivery leaders, COO, CFO, and CEO/owner are candidate buyers.
- The strongest pain is the closed-loop decision gap rather than lack of dashboards.
- Decision Memory can become a long-term product advantage.

Commercial validation, willingness to pay, exact buyer, and measurable ROI remain open.
